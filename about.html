Good food,kept simple.

Krishvi is a pure vegetarian restaurant in Jayanagar, Bengaluru. Thoughtful cooking, warm hospitality and a table that is always ready for you.

🌿
Our story

A place toslow down.

We wanted a restaurant where vegetarian food is the whole point, not the alternative.

Located in Jayanagar, Bengaluru, Krishvi brings thoughtfully crafted vegetarian cuisine, warm hospitality and an inviting atmosphere together. Whether you are here for a quick meal, a long dinner or drinks with friends, there is always a place for you.

Our cooking draws on rich Indian culinary traditions with a fresh, contemporary approach, from smoky tandoor starters to slow copper gravies and fragrant dum biryani.

🌿Come as you are. Stay as long as you like.
What we believe

Four things wenever compromise.

01

100% pure vegetarian

No meat, no fish, no compromise. The whole menu is built around vegetables, grains and spice.

02

Made by hand

Every herb, masala and marinade is ground fresh each morning, never from a packet.

03

Patience over shortcuts

Gravies simmered slowly in copper kadais. Starters charred in the tandoor and finished to order.

04

Hospitality first

Quiet lunches, long dinners and drinks with friends. Dress up or come as you are.

Inside the kitchen

Fire, copperand patience.

01 · THE TANDOOR

Charred, smoky, made to share.

Starters come off the tandoor blistered and fragrant, then are finished to order so they reach the table hot.

  • Paneer tikka with mint chutney
  • Tandoori naan, buttery and blistered
02 · THE COPPER KADAI

Slow gravies, no hurry.

Rich saffron-tomato gravies simmer patiently in copper, building depth that a quick pan never could.

  • Malai kofta in saffron-tomato gravy
  • Seasonal vegetables, cooked to order
03 · THE DUM

Layered, sealed and steamed.

Fragrant rice layered with vegetables and crisp onions, sealed and steamed until every grain carries the spice.

  • Vegetable biryani with crisp onions
  • Cooling raita on the side
