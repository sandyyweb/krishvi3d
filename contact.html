Good to know

Quickanswers.

Can’t find yours? Give us a call and we will help.

Do I need to reserve a table?

Walk-ins are always welcome. For evenings and larger groups we recommend reserving ahead, so we can have your table ready. Reserve a table.

Is everything on the menu vegetarian?

Yes. Krishvi is 100% pure vegetarian. There is no meat or fish anywhere in the kitchen or on the menu. See the menu.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 12 PM to 4 PM and 7 PM to 11 PM, for lunch and dinner.

Can you host a celebration or a group?

Yes. Tell us the occasion, the date and roughly how many of you are coming using the form above, or call us, and we will set the room around it.

How quickly will you reply?

We read every message. Calling is the fastest way to reach us during opening hours, and we reply to emails and forms as soon as we can.
